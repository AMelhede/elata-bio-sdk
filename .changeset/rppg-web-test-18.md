0.15.0-test.18: test.17 plus the later fixes from the review of the fix branches. None changes how a rate is computed from frames with a valid timestamp: on a synthetic 66 bpm face with a 10 s gap, `bpm` matched test.17 in every one of 80 seconds with the fixes off, with them on, and with them on and the pulse check, so results measured on test.17 hold. The one exception is a session that gets a frame whose timestamp is not a number (first item below), where test.17 stopped measuring.
- A frame whose timestamp is not a number no longer leaves NaN in the multi-region fuser's filters for the rest of the session: the sample grid restarts at it, as at any timestamp that does not increase. In test.17 (`realFrameRate` on, the default) the engine then got no usable sample again and kept reporting its last estimates: on a synthetic face, one such frame at 10 s left the engine's rate (`bpm` with `pulseCheck: false`, and `fused_bpm`) at 72, and its other estimates where they were, for the remaining 70 s while the true rate fell to 56 at 30 s; test.18 follows it to 56. With the pulse check on, its `bpm` followed in both builds.
- The no-reading shape (no face for a second, or an engine that failed) clears every estimate and every figure describing the signal window, as for a failed backend, instead of keeping the last window's agreement, signal quality, skin, motion and clip means and the like; the rolling baseline (which it used to clear) and calibration state are kept.
- `createRppgSession` honours a caller's `requireFace` (it was always set from the face tracking mode), and `roiSource` is null while no face is in view, so the app adapter no longer infers a face that has left.
- A WASM load that fails in `backend: "auto"` says what each candidate URL failed with (new `RppgWasmLoadError.errors`, in the order tried), so a glue file that loads while its `.wasm` does not is named instead of hidden behind the next URL's not-found.
