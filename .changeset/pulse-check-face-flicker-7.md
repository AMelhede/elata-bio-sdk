The pulse check's face flicker rule, for when no wall is in view, withholds at a brightness / colour ratio over 7 instead of 10. A 2% lamp at 90 bpm whose colour part points the way blood's does at 0.1 of its brightness swing was shown in 35 to 40 of 60 s at 10 and in none at 7 (three seeds). On recorded captures against a reference pulse, 7 and 10 show the same right and wrong seconds. On recorded captures with no reference, 7 withholds 10 of the 456 seconds that 10 showed (3 captures, reading 60 to 68), whose rightness is unknown. A lamp whose colour part is 0.15 or more of its brightness swing still passes this rule.
