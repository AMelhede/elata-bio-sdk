0.15.0-test.19: test.18 plus four changes. With every switch at its default, a session reports what test.18 reported: the test.18 audit's harness (the build's own DemoRunner, RppgProcessor and RppgSession with the real WASM in Node, synthetic faces: 66 bpm with a 10 s face gap, steady 72, no pulse with and without a gap, one timestamp that is not a number, no fusion, no engine, and 15, 20 and 60 frames a second) gave the same value in every field of every per-second read, and the same digest of every read, in all 39 runs, with the published options, the fixes off, the defaults, and the pulse check on or off. The one number that moves is the runner's `framesWithFusion` at a frame rate other than `sampleRate` (below).
- An app's own processor with neither `pushFusedSample` nor `pushSampleRgbMeta` gets its samples on the multi-region path again. In test.17 and test.18, with `realFrameRate` on (the default), a processor with only `pushSampleRgb` got 0 of 60 frames while `samplesPushed` said 60 and no error was raised (60 of 60 with the switch off). The fusion grid now uses the same push order as every other path (`pushSampleRgbMeta`, `pushSampleRgb`, `pushSample`, then a processor error). The SDK's own processors have every push method and are not affected. `framesWithFusion` counts camera frames again, not grid samples: on the synthetic face at 15 frames a second it read 2,096 for 1,050 frames in test.18 and reads 1,050; at 60 a second 2,400 for 4,800, now 4,800.
- New switch `fixes.rgbPulse`, off by default, for the processor's own colour path (an app that feeds `RppgProcessor` colour directly, or a session whose multi-region fusion is off or has no region); see its own entry. In the same harness with it on, only the runs with the fusion off change: a steady 72 reads 72 in all 64 seconds from 16 s on (0 with it off, which reads 50 and then 86), a 66 with a face gap reads within 5 in 52 of 53 shown seconds (8 of 53 off), and with no pulse at all a number shows in 62 of 79 seconds against 40 off (none at a `confidence` of 0.7 or more, against 14 off).
- README: the quick start builds when pasted into a new Vite TypeScript app; "Things to know" says that below 58 beats a minute the forehead and both cheeks must each carry the pulse; the `realFrameRate` row says what the switch changes (the fuser's filters and warm-up at a slow camera) instead of saying every rate comes out scaled: the processor reads time from the timestamps, so a slow camera changes what the fuser passes, not the scale of the rate.
