New switch `fixes.rgbPulse`, off by default: the processor's own colour path (`pushSampleRgb`, `pushSampleRgbMeta`) builds the pulse signal its window estimators read with POS and the multi-region fuser's 0.7 to 4 Hz band-pass, designed for the rate the samples arrive at (`RgbPulseExtractor`). Published, that path fed the estimators raw green for the projection's first nine samples (about a hundred grey levels beside a projected pulse of about 0.01, a step that stays in the 45 s window) and CHROM with its offset of about 1 kept, so the sample-to-sample wander of its weight lands in the heart-rate band; the estimators read neither, and an app that feeds `RppgProcessor` directly got a wrong rate (a synthetic 72 read 111 to 136 in test.17 and test.18, 105 to 115 in 0.14.0 and on Elata main). The session path is unchanged unless the fuser is off or has no region.

With it on, on a synthetic face (pulse in the blood-volume colour direction, 0.05 to 0.5 grey levels of noise, a brightness wander, 20 and 30 frames a second, 20 into a processor built for 30, timestamp jitter) the rate is within 5 of 50, 72 and 120 in 96 to 100% of seconds from 16 s on (one cell 71%, a 120 at 20 frames with ten times the noise), where the published path gets 0 to 93%. On 50 recorded captures with a reference rate, their three face regions averaged into one colour per frame, `bpm` was right (within 5) in 30% of seconds against 21% published (33 captures better, 13 worse), level with the engine's own rate (31%); the band-pass at the real rate is what does it (POS alone, unfiltered, got 13%). The rate estimate behind it (mean interval) was chosen after a run of the median-interval version on the same captures (29%), so that comparison is not an independent test. It stays off by default because with no pulse at all the estimators it repairs agree on noise more often: on synthetic noise at 30 frames a second `bpm` appears in 72% of seconds against 19% published (no change at 20 frames, 81% both). Read the way the README advises for the processor without the pulse check (show `bpm` only above a `confidence` threshold), at 0.7 none of those appear and every synthetic pulse at 0.05 and 0.2 grey levels and under the brightness wander still reads right in every second, against 0 to 19% right published (that read was not planned before the runs, so it is a lead, not a result).
