Four pulse-check rules for dim light, each OFF unless set to true in `pulseCheckRules`, so today's behaviour is unchanged: `followerMedian` (the rate shown is the median of the last 3 follower values), `reproveOnDrift` (a held rate more than 6 bpm from its last fresh proof needs a fresh proof), `tooDark` (no rate while the newest 4 windows' mean SNR is below -4 dB, the level a face with no pulse reaches, or while any of them found no line; `getPulseCheck().tooDark` says so), and `anchorDrift` (a run of shown rates ends, and the evidence starts over, once it moves more than 6 bpm from where it was first proven). Their settings were fixed before any chest-strap data existed; a chest-strap study decides whether any goes on. What each costs, from how it works: `followerMedian` makes the shown rate trail a changing heart rate by about one more second; `reproveOnDrift` and `anchorDrift` take the number away from a real heart rate that moves more than 6 bpm while shown, until the check proves it again (8 windows in normal light, 4 when the line is strong, 16 in the dark path); `tooDark` withholds right numbers too, and on generated dim faces it removed right seconds and no wrong ones (about one right second in six), and on recorded captures with a reference pulse (described only, not a test) about three right seconds for each wrong one. None is shown to work yet: no generated face reproduced the dim-light failure. `getPulseCheck()` also shows `followerBpm`, `heldBpm`, `provenBpm` and `anchorBpm` for diagnosis.
