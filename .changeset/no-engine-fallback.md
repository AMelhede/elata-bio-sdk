New switch `fixes.noEngineFallback`, off by default. When the processor's own checks refuse a rate (its low-confidence gate: the estimators disagree, the SNR is under -4.5 dB, the motion is high, or an alias with a wide spread), `bpm` falls back to the engine's raw rate, with `confidence` at most 0.3, and while the processor has under 24 samples it is the engine's raw rate outright. With the switch on, `bpm` and `fused_bpm` are null in those reads; a fresh Muse rate still shows and every read the gate passes is unchanged (paired read for read on every run below: no shown number changed value, only refused reads went blank). With the pulse check on, the default for a session, the rate shown is the check's own and does not change.

Measured with the build's real WASM core in Node, a raw processor fed colour once a frame and read once a second. With no pulse at all (sensor noise at three levels, a brightness wander, an in-band lamp flicker at 72 and 120 a minute, a pulseless capture recorded from a synthetic camera, 20 and 30 frames a second), 166 of the 477 seconds that showed a number got it from the fallback (177 of 594 with `rgbPulse` on); the switch removes exactly those. In the first second at 15 or 20 frames a second the engine's rate from 16 to 21 samples showed outright (65 for a true 72, 51 with no pulse); none of the 56 such numbers across the builds measured was right, and the switch removes them. It stays off by default by the rule written before the runs (no more than 1% of right seconds lost): on synthetic pulses at 50, 72 and 120 the fallback carried 458 of the 534 right seconds (29 of 1,701 with `rgbPulse` on), and on 50 recorded captures with a reference rate 1,300 of the 1,400 (779 of 1,967 with `rgbPulse` on). There it is right in 36% of its seconds where an engine reading its no-pulse numbers would be right in 15%: weak evidence, but evidence. The engine's own confidence does not separate its right rates from its wrong ones on those captures (median 0.60 against 0.54), so a threshold on it does not rescue the trade either.
