New pulse-check rule `darkWall` (on by default, `pulseCheckRules.darkWall: false` turns it off). When the camera's colour is too damaged to read by colour (colour damage at `OWN_PULSE_COLOUR_SWITCH` or more), the check reads the pulse from green minus the wall beside the face, which assumes the light on the face is on the wall too. Over a dark wall that is not so: a generated video with no person in it (a pulse at 70 a minute on a face lit by a screen flickering at 90, the wall beside it dark) read 88 to 91. Now the swap to green minus the wall needs the wall, as the camera reads it, at `OWN_PULSE_SWAP_MIN_WALL_TO_FACE` (0.1) of the face's brightness or more through the window's darkest tenth (`OWN_PULSE_SWAP_WALL_QUANTILE`); the ratio does not depend on the colour scale or the camera's exposure. Over a darker wall the pulse is read by colour (POS) as usual, and the same video reads 69 to 71. `WallTracker.track` (and `next`) also return `raw`, the patch as read; `PulseCheck.push` takes it as a last argument `wallRaw` (left out, `wall` is taken as read); `estimateOwnPulse` reports `wallLevel` and `wallToFace`.
