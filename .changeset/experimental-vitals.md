New option `experimentalVitals` (off by default): HRV and breathing in their own box, `session.getExperimentalVitals()` as `{ experimental: true, hrvRmssd, breathing }`, for research and testing, not for showing a person as a measurement. `breathing` is `{ rate, share }`, the breathing rate from the up-and-down motion of the chest and shoulders in a box below the chin (1.6 face widths wide, 0.15 to 1.15 face heights under the chin; each frame's shift from a one-parameter Lucas-Kanade fit, resampled at 4 Hz, band-passed 0.1 to 0.6 Hz, the strongest line over 32 s); a window needs a sample in 90% of its 1 s stretches, the motion is dropped after a second with no chest box or no frames, and no rate is given while frames have stopped. It is separate from the metrics' `respiration_rate`. `hrvRmssd` comes from the session's latest `getMetrics()` read, only while a face is in view and the pulse check (when on) still shows the rate it proved; with the pulse check off, the engine's own HRV. With the pulse check on, `getMetrics()` and `getDebugSnapshot()` withhold HRV and breathing whether this option is on or off; `session.processor`, the raw engine, is not filtered. `DemoRunner.msSinceAnalysisRestart()` reports the time since the face came back after an absence long enough to restart the signal.
