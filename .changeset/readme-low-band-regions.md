The README's "Things to know" says that below 58 beats a minute the pulse check needs the forehead and both cheeks to carry the pulse, while above it two of the three are enough, so a person resting under 58 whose forehead cannot be read gets no number. A fresh app showed it on a generated face whose forehead had no pulse: 70 after 24 s, 50 never in 120 s. A test keeps the two numbers in the README equal to the code's.
